Bubble Craps — Excluded Jurisdictions

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

The Bubble Craps Sweepstakes (Sweeps Coins play and redemption) is not available to residents of, or individuals physically located in, the jurisdictions listed below. Residents of excluded jurisdictions may download and use Bubble Craps for Gold Coins play only; they may not acquire, play with, or redeem Sweeps Coins. The list is updated from time to time to reflect changes in applicable law.

Excluded U.S. States

California (CA)
Connecticut (CT)
Florida (FL)
Hawaii (HI)
Idaho (ID)
Michigan (MI)
Montana (MT)
Nevada (NV)
New York (NY)
Rhode Island (RI)
Utah (UT)
Washington (WA)

In addition, the Sweepstakes is void in any other U.S. state, territory, or jurisdiction where the Sweepstakes is prohibited by law, or where it would require registration, licensing, or bonding that Tappy Games has not completed.

Excluded Countries

The Sweepstakes is available only in the United States. It is not available in any other country, including without limitation Canada, the United Kingdom, any member state of the European Union, Australia, New Zealand, or any jurisdiction outside the United States.

Age Restrictions

In addition to jurisdictional exclusions, all participants must be at least 21 years of age to participate in or redeem Sweeps Coins.

Enforcement

Tappy Games enforces these exclusions through IP-based geolocation, device-level GPS (precise location) required for Sweeps Coins play, billing address cross-check at KYC, VPN and proxy detection, self-declared state of residence at sign-up, and mailing-address verification on AMOE requests. A participant located in or a resident of an excluded jurisdiction is ineligible regardless of self-declared address. Attempts to circumvent geographic restrictions (including by VPN or proxy) violate the Terms of Use and result in forfeiture of Sweeps Coins.

Changes

This list may be updated at Tappy Games' discretion. Material changes will be published on this page and in-app. If you are unsure whether you are in an eligible jurisdiction, contact support@tappygames.com.
