5. How to Enter

There are two methods of entry. No purchase is necessary to enter or win.

5.1 Method 1 — Gameplay Entry

Sweeps Coins ("SC") are sweepstakes entries. You may obtain SC at no additional cost through:

  • Free bonus SC with Gold Coins purchases. When you purchase Gold Coins, Sponsor may include a free bonus grant of SC. The SC is always included at no additional cost; the Gold Coins purchase price reflects only the Gold Coins themselves.
  • Daily login bonus. A free grant of SC is available by logging in daily, subject to limits published in the Service.
  • Promotional grants. Sponsor may offer additional free SC through promotions, tournaments, and special events.
  • Other free methods Sponsor may introduce from time to time.

Once you hold SC, you may use it to play Bubble Craps in Sweeps Coins mode. Each bet placed with SC is an entry into the Sweepstakes for that round. Outcomes are determined by the random roll of two fair, randomized six-sided dice.

5.2 Method 2 — Alternative Method of Entry ("AMOE")

Participants may obtain SC without making any purchase or incurring any cost by submitting a handwritten mail-in AMOE request as described in the AMOE Procedure. The AMOE provides SC equivalent in value to the bonus SC grant included with Sponsor's smallest Gold Coins purchase package. AMOE requests are limited to one (1) per person per day. Mail to: Tappy Games, LLC, Attn: Bubble Craps AMOE Requests, PO Box 15, Saint Helens, OR 97051.

A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. The random outcome of dice rolls is the same for SC obtained through gameplay entry and SC obtained through the AMOE.

6. Odds

Outcomes are determined by the random roll of two six-sided dice. The mathematical odds of winning for each bet type are inherent to the rules of the game of craps and are published in-app. For example, the probability of rolling a 7 on any given roll is 6 in 36 (approximately 16.67%). The odds of winning any given entry depend on the specific bet placed. Sponsor uses a cryptographically secure random number generator.
