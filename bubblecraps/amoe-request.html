Free Sweeps Coins — AMOE Entry

Bubble Craps offers a Free Alternative Method of Entry (AMOE) so you can obtain Sweeps Coins without making any purchase.

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED BY LAW.

Mail-In Only

AMOE requests are accepted by U.S. Postal Mail only. We do not accept web, email, or in-app submissions. The mail-in requirement keeps the alternative entry method genuine and equally available to every participant, and prevents automated abuse.

How to Submit

Handwrite (do not type or print from a computer) the following on a single 3 × 5 inch index card or sheet of paper:

  1. "Bubble Craps SC Request" as the subject line.
  2. Your full legal first and last name.
  3. Your date of birth.
  4. Your complete residential mailing address.
  5. Your email address.
  6. The username or account email associated with your Bubble Craps account.
  7. Your original signature.
  8. The handwritten statement: "I am at least 21 years old and wish to receive Sweeps Coins in the Bubble Craps sweepstakes."

Place the completed card in an envelope with sufficient postage and mail it to:

Tappy Games, LLC
Attn: Bubble Craps AMOE Requests
PO Box 15
Saint Helens, OR 97051
United States

Important Rules

  • Handwritten only. Mechanically reproduced, photocopied, mass-produced, or illegible requests will be rejected.
  • One entry per outer envelope. Multiple entries in one envelope will be treated as a single entry.
  • One entry per person per day. Additional entries above the daily limit will be disregarded.
  • 21 or older, US residents only. Excluded states: WA, ID, NV, MI, and any state where prohibited.
  • Processing time: within 14 days after Sponsor's receipt of a valid, complete request.

SC Grant

Each successfully processed AMOE request results in a grant of Sweeps Coins equivalent in value to the bonus SC included with our smallest Gold Coins purchase package. AMOE-granted SC is fully equivalent in value and redemption rights to SC obtained any other way.
