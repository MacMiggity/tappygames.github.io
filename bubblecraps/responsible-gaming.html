1. Our Commitments

  • We provide self-service tools to help you limit time and spending.
  • We offer confidential self-exclusion for users who need a break.
  • We surface responsible-gaming resources prominently within the Service.
  • We train our customer-support team to recognize signs of problem play.
  • We do not target marketing to users who have self-excluded or shown signs of problem play.
  • We comply with responsible-gaming standards required by applicable law.

2. User-Controlled Tools

You can adjust the following in the Service's Settings → Responsible Gaming screen:

2.1 Deposit Limits

Set a daily, weekly, or monthly cap on Gold Coins purchases. Limits apply at the point of purchase; purchases beyond the limit are blocked until the next period. Limits can be decreased immediately; increased or removed only after a 24-hour cooling-off period.

2.2 Session-Time Limits

Set a daily session-duration cap. When reached, the Service will prompt you to take a break and log you out of Sweeps Coins play for the remainder of the day.

2.3 Loss Limits

Set a weekly cap on net Sweeps Coins loss. When reached, you will be temporarily blocked from SC play for the remainder of the period.

2.4 Reality-Check Notifications

Configure a recurring pop-up reminder (every 30, 60, or 90 minutes) showing elapsed session time and net result. Default is every 60 minutes for SC sessions longer than 60 minutes.

2.5 Activity Dashboard

View your full transaction history — purchases, grants (bonus, daily, AMOE), wagers, wins, losses, and redemptions — in Settings → My Activity.

3. Self-Exclusion

You may self-exclude at any time from Settings → Responsible Gaming → Self-Exclude.

3.1 Exclusion periods

  • Short cool-off: 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. Reversible only after the period ends.
  • Medium: 6 months. Reversible only after a cooling-off reactivation step.
  • Long: 1 year, 5 years.
  • Permanent. Irreversible.

3.2 Effects of self-exclusion

During self-exclusion you are locked out of the Service, including Gold Coins play, Sweeps Coins play, and redemptions. Pending redemptions initiated before self-exclusion will be processed to completion, subject to KYC and eligibility. You will not receive promotional emails or push notifications. We do not create replacement accounts for you. Attempts to re-register under a different identity violate the Terms of Use.

3.3 Data retention during self-exclusion

We retain identifying information sufficient to enforce self-exclusion even if you request account deletion. This is a narrow exception to deletion rights, disclosed in our Privacy Policy.

3.4 Reactivation

After a non-permanent period ends, reactivation requires a written request from the account owner, a 72-hour cooling-off period, and acknowledgment of this Policy and the Terms of Use. Permanent self-exclusion is irreversible.

3.5 Third-party self-exclusion

Where required by law, we honor state-administered self-exclusion lists. If you are on a state self-exclusion list covering lotteries, casinos, or sports betting in a jurisdiction where we operate, you should not use the Sweeps Coins features.

4. Minimum Age

You must be at least 18 years old to use Bubble Craps, and at least 21 years old to play with or redeem Sweeps Coins. We enforce this through self-declared date of birth at sign-up, verified government-issued ID during KYC, and behavioral signals reviewed by our compliance team. If we determine an underage person has used the Service, we will close the account, reverse Gold Coins purchases to the extent possible, and forfeit Sweeps Coins.

Parents and guardians: if you believe a minor has used the Service, contact support@tappygames.com.

5. Identifying Problem Play

Signs that may indicate a gambling problem include:

  • Spending more time or money on games than you intended.
  • Chasing losses by betting more to recover.
  • Lying to family or friends about time or money spent.
  • Borrowing money to play or to pay related debts.
  • Feeling restless, irritable, or anxious when not playing.
  • Neglecting work, school, or relationships.

If any of these apply to you or someone you care about, help is available below.

6. How We Support Responsible Play

  • Advertising: we do not advertise to users who have self-excluded and do not direct marketing to minors.
  • Promotions: bonuses are optional and cannot compel continued play. Wagering requirements are clearly disclosed.
  • Customer support: trained on responsible-gaming principles. If you contact us about a play-related concern, we will provide this Policy, resources, and help setting limits or self-exclusion.
  • Account reviews: we may proactively reach out, temporarily restrict activity, or require acknowledgments when our systems flag patterns consistent with problem play.
  • No reverse withdrawals: pending redemptions cannot be canceled to re-wager the amount.
