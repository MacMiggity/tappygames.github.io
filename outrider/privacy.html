Raptor Run Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 8, 2026

Short version: Raptor Run does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your settings and game progress are stored only on your device.

About this policy

This policy describes how Tappy Games LLC ("Tappy Games," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in the Raptor Run mobile game, also developed under the project name Outrider.

Information collection

Raptor Run does not require an account and does not collect personal information, device identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, audio, analytics, advertising data, or gameplay telemetry. The game does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Local game data

Game progress, unlocked content, preferences, and settings are saved locally on your device so the game can function. This data is not sent to Tappy Games. Removing the app may remove this locally stored data, subject to your device and backup settings.

Network access and purchases

Raptor Run is designed to play without an account or an internet connection. The current version does not offer in-app purchases and does not process payments.

Children's privacy

Because Raptor Run does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

External links

The game may open this privacy policy or the support page in your web browser. Those websites are governed by their own browser and hosting-provider practices. No gameplay data is added to these links.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before the changed version is released.

Contact

Questions about Raptor Run privacy can be sent to adam@tappygames.com.
